# Part 1
“He has gone! But has he broken it off with her?” she thought. “Can it be he sees her? Why didn’t I ask him! No, no, reconciliation is impossible. Even if we remain in the same house, we are strangers—strangers forever!” She repeated again with special significance the word so dreadful to her. “And how I loved him! My God, how I loved him!… How I loved him! And now don’t I love him? Don’t I love him more than before? The most horrible thing …” she began, but did not finish her thought, because Matryona Filimonovna put her head in at the door.

For me to live with him now would be torture, just because I love my past love for him …”

“Remember, Anna, what you’ve done for me—I shall never forget. And remember that I love you, and shall always love you as my dearest friend!”
